What we make

DecentAI is a platform for running AI agents under an organization's own control. It runs on your own computer or in your own environment; every agent is reviewed before it is installed, checked on every call, and recorded.

  • The platform and its web client, source-available and free for individuals and small businesses: see Download.
  • Agents, built to the platform's published standard and offered in public catalogs: see Agents.
  • The apps for iPhone, Android, Windows and macOS, which connect to a DecentAI server, coming soon. See Support and the privacy policy.

Why

AI agents are most useful where the work is: in mail, documents and the systems an organization already runs. That is also where they can do the most harm. DecentAI is built so an organization decides which agents exist, who may use them and what each action may do, and can read afterwards exactly what happened. Nothing about its people, conversations or data passes through us.

How we work

The platform, the web client and the agents are developed in the open on GitHub, where anyone can read the code, report a problem or propose a change. Security reports go through the security policy.
