What it runs on

Your computer, or your own servers.

DecentAI runs with Docker and Compose, on Windows, macOS or Linux.

The platform

People, agents, records and chats, the sandbox agents run in, and the audit trail. On one computer, served to it only, or on your servers or cloud account. One installation can serve several organizations, each kept apart.

The web client

The app people use in a browser. Static files, served beside the platform or from any host you choose.

Your AI model

One of over two hundred providers, or a model you host yourself. You hold the keys.

Agents

None at first. You add the ones you need from catalogs, each after reading what it declares.

Agents

Open source, for everyone.

The project's agents are under Apache-2.0, in two public catalogs. In DecentAI, open Agents, then Marketplace, and add a source with either address. No credential is needed.

https://raw.githubusercontent.com/decentai-io/decentai-official-agents/main/catalog/catalog.json
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/decentai-io/decentai-browser-agent/main/catalog/catalog.json

The first is thirty-seven agents for mail, calendars, files, documents and records; the second, a real web browser. What each one does.
