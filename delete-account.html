Your data

Delete your DecentAI account or data

For the DecentAI app for iPhone and Android (Google Play developer: DECENT INTELLIGENCE ARTIFICIAL SERVICES), and the DecentAI desktop app.

Where your account is

Your DecentAI account, chats, files and records are kept on the DecentAI server you sign in to, not by us. That server is run by your organization, or by you if you set it up. Its administrator can delete your account; you can delete your own chats, files and records in the app at any time.

Delete your account

  1. Ask your DecentAI server's administrator to remove you. In the app, an administrator does this on the People page (in the You tab): open the person and choose Remove them.
  2. If you made the organization yourself, or cannot reach its administrator, email support@decentai.io from the address you sign in with, and name the server's address. We help you reach whoever runs that server, or, for a server we run, delete the account ourselves within 30 days.
  3. Sign out of the app (You → Sign out) and remove it from your phone. This deletes everything the app kept on the phone.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted: your sign-in and profile (name and email address), your chats, schedules, memories, private passwords and keys, and the phones registered for notifications.
  • Handed on: files and records you made, and what you shared with others, pass to the person the administrator names, so the organization's work is not lost.
  • Kept for a set time: the organization's audit log, which records who did what, and the record of agents' work, for the number of days the server's administrator sets. Backups the administrator made earlier keep what they held until they are replaced.

Delete some of your data, and keep your account

  1. A chat: in Chats, swipe or long-press the chat and choose Delete.
  2. Files: on the Files page, select them and choose Delete.
  3. Records an agent keeps for you: on the Records page, open one's menu and choose Delete. Memory and Secrets and accounts delete the same way.
  4. Anything else, or everything at once: ask your server's administrator, or write to support@decentai.io as above.

What you delete is removed from the server at once. The audit log, the record of agents' work and earlier backups are kept as described above. See also the privacy policy.
