Agents

Agents for the work you already do.

Thirty-eight agents published by the DecentAI project, free and open source. Each one is installed only after your administrator reads what it declares, and does nothing else.

Add them to your DecentAI

Two catalogs, no credential needed.

In DecentAI, open Agents, then Marketplace, choose Add a source and paste a catalog's address. Then review each agent and install the ones you want.

Accounts you connect

Sign in once; the platform keeps the tokens.

The agent gets a fresh access token when it reads the account, never your password. Anything that sends, books or shares waits for your go-ahead.
